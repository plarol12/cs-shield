Moving Target Defense in SDN-enabled EV Charging Networks

You are the attacker. Tap the charger 5 times in a row. Every time you find it, CS-SHIELD gives it a new address.

Find the charger
10.0.2.14

You found it — and it moved every time

That is moving target defense: whatever the attacker learns about the chargers goes stale after each shuffle. In our tests, CS-SHIELD detected and stopped a low-rate DoS attack in 17.9 ms with all chargers staying online.
